# ASX Penny-Stock Research Roadmap

**Goal:** Progress from 318 provisionally classified ASX operating-company candidates to 5–10 deeply researched opportunities over a 1–2-year investment horizon. Intended portfolio budget: under A$10,000. This is a research process, not a recommendation to buy.

## Ordered next steps

1. **Validate the 318 operating companies.** Confirm active ASX listings, remove duplicates and misclassified investment vehicles, and flag suspended or delisted securities.
2. **Resolve missing market data.** Investigate 196 securities with missing price histories and remaining missing market capitalisations; add any eligible operating companies.
3. **Collect financial statements.** Retrieve annual, half-year and applicable quarterly cash-flow reports; record cash, revenue, operating cash flow, debt, shares outstanding, source dates and links.
4. **Calculate financial health.** Assess cash runway, revenue growth, profitability, debt and likely funding needs, using sector-specific measures for explorers, biotech and established businesses.
5. **Screen for financial resilience.** Narrow toward 100–150 candidates, with documented exceptions for early-stage companies with credible milestones.
6. **Assess dilution.** Review historical issuance, placements, convertibles, options and expected future capital raisings.
7. **Identify catalysts in the next 3–24 months.** Include exploration results, approvals, trials, contracts, production milestones and product launches; record uncertainty.
8. **Shortlist 30–50 companies.** Prioritise resilience, catalysts, execution, valuation, liquidity and dilution, not momentum alone.
9. **Deep-research 15–20.** Examine management, competition, project economics, shareholders, filings and execution history.
10. **Value 5–10 finalists.** Prepare bear/base/bull scenarios including financing and dilution assumptions.
11. **Propose portfolio construction.** Evaluate sizing, liquidity, diversification, thesis-invalidating events and exits within the under-A$10,000 budget.
12. **Implement monitoring.** Use GitHub Actions and persistent storage to refresh market data, track ASX announcements and capital raisings, and flag thesis changes.

## Current status

- Initial ASX universe: 1,923 securities.
- Numerical screening: 339 qualified.
- Provisional classification: 318 operating candidates, 20 investment vehicles excluded, 1 uncertain.
- 196 missing-price records remain to be investigated.
- **Immediate next action: Step 1 — validate the 318 operating companies.**

## Quality controls

- Retain sources, observation dates and original datasets.
- Treat missing values as unknown, not zero.
- Verify corporate actions and shares on issue before valuation.
- Review results at each stage before proceeding.
