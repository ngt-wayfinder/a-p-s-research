# Evidence standards

## Hierarchy
1. Dated issuer-authored ASX disclosures, official exchange records, statutory company registers and signed issuer financial reports.
2. Official regulatory and relevant overseas exchange documents.
3. Vendor/aggregator data and media for discovery or corroboration, **not** sole proof of identity, security form or trading status.

## Required evidence fields
ASX code; legal issuer; security class; primary URL; document title and publication date; specific claim and section/page; review date; known limitations; associated register/ledger evidence ID.

## Independent verification gates
- **Issuer identity:** legal entity and ticker mapped to dated primary evidence.
- **Security:** quoted class, options/CDIs and ratios, and material corporate actions reconciled for metrics used.
- **Trading:** dated halt, suspension, resumption and quotation evidence; absence of a recent price is not proof.
- **Operating classification:** issuer activity and vehicle/REIT exclusions supported by primary disclosure.
- **Continuity:** historical name/code changes, mergers/RTOs, consolidations and dilution addressed to the level relevant to analysis.

Level 1 is minimum screening eligibility; Level 2 extends history for survivors; Level 3 is investment-grade reconciliation for finalists. A material unresolved conflict means `QUARANTINED`; lack of review means `REVIEW_PENDING`. Do not assert live tradeability from an old document.
