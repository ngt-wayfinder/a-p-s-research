## Method and limits

Appendix 2A Part 1 names, expressly typed identifiers and issuer tickers were compared with freshly read ABR identity fields. Where no current form was available, issuer corporate directories and official ASX archive linkage were examined. Annual/interim reports were read for legal entity, incorporation disclosures and material name or accounting changes. Page-specific facts and source URLs are in the ledger. A source-supported name/ticker/identifier conclusion does not establish an entire issuer history.

All 20 substantiated issuer ABNs pass arithmetic checks, which are not identity proof. Unsubstantiated identifiers remain unresolved; any report/registry discrepancies stay visible. Existing matrix/register schemas are unchanged; the new batch CSV explicitly includes ARBN, foreign reference and ISIN fields for substantiated facts. Generic registry numbers are not automatically typed as ACNs. Parent identifiers are distinguished from auditor and subsidiary identifiers. Dated quotation applications are not proof of completed quotation processing or current trading. Unknown fields remain unknown. Prior ticker/history gaps and manual-review flags remain preserved.
