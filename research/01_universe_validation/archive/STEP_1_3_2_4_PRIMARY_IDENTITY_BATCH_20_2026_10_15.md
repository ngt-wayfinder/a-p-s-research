## Primary-source method and acceptance

Each selected issuer has a dated issuer/ASX source establishing legal name,ticker and explicit ACN/ARBN or foreign registration mapping. Original PDF text was read; sources requiring a web fallback were opened as complete original documents. Government registry records corroborate identifiers and expose registry discrepancies. ACNs were not inferred from ABN suffixes. Australian incorporation is supported by issuer reports; addresses and subsidiary domiciles were not used to assign jurisdiction.

73 fresh source entries were appended to the canonical evidence ledger,IDs E01185–E01257. Seventy local source receipts were rehashed by the consolidating auditor; three original-document web reads have no fabricated local PDF hash. Source receipt paths are transient audit metadata, not repository links; public original URLs and hashes provide reproducibility. ABR observations dated15 October are corroboration on the review date, not a backdated certification of13 October registry state.

## Material qualifications and source corrections

- SOP: issuer annual report explicitly establishes Bermuda incorporation and the ASX form explicitly labels ARBN 161803032. ABR uses an Australian PublicCompany label; retain the source conflict and use the direct issuer incorporation evidence. No Australian ACN was inferred.
- TOK: Tolu Minerals Limited is PNG-incorporated; issuer annual report states PNG registration 1-125888 and the ASX form labels ARBN 657300359. An Australian ABN was not established in this reviewed source set.
- TRE: original Ontario incorporation and completed Australian continuance effective8 January2024 are separately documented. ACN 661082435 is explicit in the issuer offer booklet. A proposed CDI timetable is not proof of each final implementation step.
- TVN: actual issuer evidence supports TNG Limited→Tivan Limited/TVN effective25 January2023. The explicit ACN 000817023 was read in an issuer prospectus,not derived from ABN.
- VBC: earlier issuer proposal namedVBR; the subsequent implementation notice establishesVBC effective10 December2020 and ASICname processing3 December2020. ABR8 January2021 is a registry observation date, not the ASXeffective date.
- TOR: the freshly read and visuallychecked March2026 half-year report did not support the inherited claim that it printed ABN 44621122905. The actual ABR record independently establishes that ABN. The attribution error is preserved as a source correction.
- TAM: explicit ACN 000617176 is corroborated by issuer prospectus and AGM sources; selected2013/2023/2026 continuity points do not amount to complete legal history.
