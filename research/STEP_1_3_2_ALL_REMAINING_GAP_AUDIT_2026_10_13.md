1. Inspect issuer-authored dated ASX filing; record ACN/ARBN, legal entity, security class, document date and direct source URL.
2. Trace ticker changes, historical code reuse, mergers, demergers and consolidations through effective dates; verify actual allotments rather than announced proposals.
3. Confirm any removal, suspension or reinstatement with official notices; distinguish historical events from current Step 1.2 trading eligibility.
4. Update each dimension in the 316-company evidence matrix only after inspection. Do not treat initial triage, announcement headlines or a third-party mirror as final verification.
