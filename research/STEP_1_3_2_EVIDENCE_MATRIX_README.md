# Issuer evidence matrix — interpretation

The accompanying CSV covers all 316 candidates as of 13 October 2026. It merges the 279-row issuer continuity queue with 37 names handled in a separate name-continuity review.

`EVIDENCE_RECORDED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` means the queue mentions issuer or ASX material; it is **not** a verification conclusion. `PRIMARY_SOURCE_PENDING` means initial triage has not resolved primary-source evidence. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` includes ambiguous entries and the separate 37 names; inspect their original review before classifying. `NOT_FULLY_VERIFIED` means the specified historical-continuity or security-form gate has not been demonstrated by this matrix. `STEP_1_2_REQUIRED` means this exercise did not establish current tradeability.

The Existing_Research_Note field preserves the queue's wording and source links. It can contain older information, proposed transactions and third-party sources; reviewers must inspect cited documents and dates before upgrading statuses.

## Required closure workflow

1. Validate a dated issuer-authored ASX filing for each row and record legal issuer, ACN/ARBN, security type, document date and source URL.
2. Check historical ticker reuse, name changes, demergers, mergers and effective dates; do not splice distinct issuers.
3. Verify CDI ratios, options, share consolidations and adjusted-versus-raw historical market data.
4. Check Step 1.2 dated trading and suspension status separately.
5. Mark each verification dimension complete only with an auditable source and reviewer note.

This is a tracking baseline, not completion of Step 1.3.2. No investment ranking or buy recommendation is implied.
